## What this is

Gracious Quotes is a quotes site, started in February 2020 and rebuilt in 2026 around accurate attribution.
Many popular quotes are credited to the wrong person, or were never said at all. Every quote we have checked
carries a verdict, the reason for it, and the source we traced it to.

## How a quote gets its verdict

A quote is only **verified** when we have found it in the person's own work, or in a reliable source that cites it,
with a page, chapter, date or timestamp. Anything less is labelled for what it is:

| Verdict | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Verified** | Found in these words in the original book, speech, letter or interview, or in a reliable source that cites it, with the work named. |
| **Plausible** | Consistently credited to this person in reliable references, but the original wording has not been located yet. |
| **Disputed** | Sources disagree, the line appears only in quote collections or social posts, or it first shows up in print long after the person lived. |
| **Re-credited** | Someone else said or wrote it first. We name who, and the work, or say plainly that the real author is unknown. |
| **Traditional saying** | A proverb or common saying with no known author, labelled honestly rather than pinned on a famous name. |

The method, in short:

- **Search the exact wording** in full-text book archives, punctuation intact.
- **Go to the person's own work first**: their books, letters, speeches and interviews come before anyone else's collection.
- **Date the first appearance.** A line that first appears in print decades after someone died is disputed.
- **Respect the translation.** We show the original language where we have it and name the translator.
- **Record the verdict with its reason and source.** When a verdict changes, the change and the date go on the public corrections log.

Live counts for each verdict, and a worked example traced from claim to source, are on
[How we source quotes](https://graciousquotes.com/how-we-source-quotes/).

## Open data

Every checked quote is listed with its verdict and source in [Quotes With Sources](https://graciousquotes.com/quotes-with-sources/),
and the same records are available as a read-only JSON feed, no key needed:

```
GET https://graciousquotes.com/wp-json/gq/v1/quotes
GET https://graciousquotes.com/wp-json/gq/v1/quotes/{id}
```

The pages and the feed are built from the same records, so they cannot disagree. Endpoints, terms and examples: [For developers](https://graciousquotes.com/developers/).

## Use of AI

Technology and AI help us search and organise. They do not replace human judgement and source checking:
a person reviews every attribution and approves every correction.

## Found a mistake?

Some quotes have genuinely uncertain origins, and we will not claim every one is settled.
If you can show a better source, [tell us](https://graciousquotes.com/contact/). Every confirmed change is dated and explained on the
[corrections log](https://graciousquotes.com/corrections/).
